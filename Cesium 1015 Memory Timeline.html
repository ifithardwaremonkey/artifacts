Rivendell is killed at 71.4 s, after the last sample.

Interesting things

Caveat on clocks: all times are the device clock (CDT) read from the bugreport logs. The regression run's start (09:36:22) is inferred from its file name; the lmkd kill times line up with the stall in its data, but Bryan has not confirmed the run's start and end times or its pass/fail result. The leak rate is the average over uptime (objects ÷ seconds), not a measured slope on this unit. MiB = 1,048,576 bytes. Memory series come from the framework's am_meminfo event (bytes), a coarser measure than /proc/slabinfo; the two agree on the trend, not on exact values. Per-app logs (rivendell, gandalf, glassos_service, eru) and their archives were not read. Skin temperature was not reported in the regression CSV.
