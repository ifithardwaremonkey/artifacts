Cesium 0909 — this boot's timeline (5 d 9 h, mostly idle)

Device clock (CDT). From the last power-on, 10-06 07:14:43 (an automatic hang_detect reboot), to the 10-11 16:43 bugreport (uptime 5 d 9 h 28 m). Sources: three bugreports (10-09 10:42, 10-10 22:40 and 10-11 16:43) and their system logs, event logs and ActivityManager memory samples, merged. Two short vmstat 1 runs (10-10 22:54 and 10-12 00:45) are described in the cards. Nothing here is from the earlier boot except where stated.

A. Memory by category, sampled by ActivityManager every ~3 h

The kernel's share grows steadily; free RAM drifts down. Dashed orange lines are straight-line fits through the kernel samples before and after the 10-09 session. Across that session both the kernel figure and the cached figure step (kernel up, cached down) while slab accounting shows no step. The cached series is the framework's own derived number, not /proc Cached: the kernel's real page cache was 1,005 MiB at the 10-09 10:42 bugreport and 962 MiB at the 10-10 22:40 one, so no 500 MiB of page cache was lost. The dotted grey line is /proc Cached at the three bugreports (1,005, 962 and 878 MiB): the real page cache is being squeezed slowly as slab grows. Use these series as a trend check and the slab numbers as the primary evidence. Hollow marks were sampled while a bugreport was running and are left out of the lines and the fits.

E. Zoom: the three gandalf kills, 10-09 21:30 to 23:30

Each kill is logged by ActivityManager as "Async binder space running out while frozen". Every restart lands exactly on an eru heartbeat (:12:30 or :42:30), is frozen about a minute later, and is killed about 15 to 16 minutes after that heartbeat. The fourth instance (23:12:30) was not frozen for 24.5 h and was killed on 10-10 (panel F). At each kill the failed-transaction log shows three oneway sends of about 1.1 KB from ConnectivityService (system_server thread 1249) to three different binder nodes in gandalf, all refused for lack of buffer space; gandalf holds five network callbacks. Earlier freezes of up to 29 minutes (10-07 to 10-08) were survived.

F. Zoom: the fourth gandalf kill, 10-10 23:30 to 10-11 00:30

Pid 11404 had run since 10-09 23:12:30 without ever being frozen. After the 23:42:32 eru heartbeat it was frozen at 23:43:11 (41 s later) and killed at 23:59:31.928, 16.3 minutes after the freeze, with three failed oneway sends (1100, 1112 and 1100 bytes) from ConnectivityService to three binder nodes in gandalf. At 00:12:32 the next heartbeat started a new instance (pid 27072), which has not been frozen through 10-11 16:43. All four freezes since 10-09 21:43 were fatal; the eight earlier ones (up to 29 min) were survived.

Interesting things

Caveat on clocks: the first ~20 s of every boot are stamped with the last NTP time the device saved (here 10-05 21:38), which the VKC1_20260831 build keeps as a lower bound so the clock does not restart at epoch, until NTP steps the clock at 07:14:58. Those lines are folded into "boot". MiB = 1,048,576 bytes. Memory figures come from the framework's am_meminfo event (bytes), which is a coarser measure than /proc/slabinfo; the two agree on the trend, not on exact values.
