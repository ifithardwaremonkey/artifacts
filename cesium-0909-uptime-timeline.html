Interesting things

Caveat on clocks: this device has no battery-backed RTC, so the first ~20 s of every boot are stamped with a stale saved time (here 10-05 21:38) until NTP corrects it at 07:14:58. Those lines are folded into "boot". MiB = 1,048,576 bytes. Memory figures come from the framework's am_meminfo event (bytes), which is a coarser measure than /proc/slabinfo; the two agree on the trend, not on exact values.
